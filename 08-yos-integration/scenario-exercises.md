# Scenario exercises (draft, from FiF "Think Tank")

**Status:** DRAFT, not canon. **Provenance:** FiF prototype spec (January 2026).

1. **Scenario Builder** — write a personal or organizational future scenario: starting point, one transformation domain, three drivers, two plausible outcomes, signals to watch.
2. **Implications Engine** — "If X happens, then Y…": chain first-, second- and third-order effects across at least two domains.
3. **Futures meditation** — a guided visualization of 2030, 2040 and 2050: what a normal day looks like, what has disappeared, what has become precious.
4. **Weekly Signals** — a short weekly digest of developments per domain, each labelled *already happening*, *emerging* or *speculative*.

Reflection sessions are timed (e.g. 10 / 20 min). Outputs may be kept in a journal; nothing is shared by default.
